# Product photography — the watch and the tracker

Twenty-two source photos of the hardware, as received over WhatsApp. Kept
because they are the only product imagery in the repo and the storefront and
landing both need them; the landing currently ships its own exported assets, so
nothing references these yet.

They are originals, not web assets: full-resolution, unretouched, and named the
way WhatsApp named them. Crop and compress a copy before putting one on a page —
do not link these directly.

(Committed unintentionally alongside the design-system work; kept deliberately
after review rather than left as mystery files. The voice note that arrived with
them, docs/audio/2.m4a, was removed — a recording of a conversation is not
source material.)
